    The job hunt dataset

    Each check of your own numbers adds one row to an anonymous dataset about job hunts. The row holds your start date, applications, interviews and offers, plus your answers about your search if you gave any. The dataset stores no name, email address, IP address, cookie or ID, so a row cannot be traced back to you. The example numbers are never added.

    To opt out, tick Don't add my numbers under Check my numbers. This browser remembers the choice. Legal basis: GDPR Art. 6(1)(f), a legitimate interest in understanding how job hunts go and improving this tool.

    How the read works

    Typical rate. Across about two million applications tracked by Huntr, 3% to 6% led to an interview (about 3% with an untailored CV, about 6% with a tailored one). The data is mostly from the US and self-reported, so your field and country may differ.

    Replies take time. Interview invites usually arrive within one to three weeks. Applications from the last few weeks have not had their full chance yet, so they count only partly. The tool assumes your applications were spread evenly over your days of applying.

    Your rate. The estimate starts from "any rate is possible" and updates with your numbers (a Beta-Binomial model). The range covers your true rate with 95% probability.

    The fortune comes from the chance that your true rate is below 3%. Under 50% draws 吉 "Normal ghosting", 50% to 80% 末吉 "A little haunted", 80% to 95% 凶 "Ghost town" and above 95% 大凶 "Haunted market?". A rate likely above typical draws 大吉 "Ghost-free". The worse the fortune, the more yokai come out.

    What it cannot tell you. One person's numbers cannot separate a bad market from a CV or targeting problem. If you land in the last band, check the roles you pick and how your CV reads first, because that is the part you control.
